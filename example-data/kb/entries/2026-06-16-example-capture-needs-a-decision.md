**Thesis** — A note system stays in use only if every capture forces one decision ("what is this
for?"); systems that store without asking turn into archives nobody reopens.

**So what (for my work)** — This is the bet behind `example-project`'s triage step: the cost of
asking at capture is worth paying. Opens `example-theme` at parking.

**Key evidence**
- The author's own system: re-reads rose after adding a one-line "why I saved this" field (a
  self-report — no numbers given).
- Names the failure mode: saving is cheap, so volume grows faster than review time.
- Counter-case the author doesn't address: people who search rather than browse their notes.

**What stuck** — The decision at capture is the product; storage is a commodity.
